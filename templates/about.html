About FilmiFire

India's dedicated cinema news and box office platform

Who We Are

FilmiFire is a passionate, independent Indian cinema news platform dedicated to covering box office collections, records, and film news from every major Indian film industry — Bollywood, Kollywood, Tollywood, Mollywood, and Sandalwood.

We started FilmiFire because we believed Indian cinema fans deserved a single, reliable destination for accurate box office data, honest analysis, and timely news — covering not just Bollywood but every industry that makes Indian cinema the most vibrant film culture in the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple — bring every Indian film industry under one roof. We cover the biggest Rajinikanth release with the same energy we bring to a Bollywood blockbuster or a Malayalam surprise hit. Every industry matters. Every record deserves to be documented.

What We Cover

  • Box Office Collections — Day-by-day, week-by-week breakdowns of India's biggest films
  • Records — Every milestone, every all-time record, every number that matters
  • Upcoming Films — Previews, advance booking analysis, and release updates
  • Industry News — Casting announcements, production updates, OTT releases
  • Comparisons — How today's films stack up against the all-time greats

Our Commitment

We are committed to accuracy. Box office numbers in Indian media are often inflated or unverified. At FilmiFire, we cross-reference multiple trade sources before publishing any collection figure. When numbers are estimates or projections, we say so clearly.

We cover all industries without bias — no favourite star, no favourite industry. The numbers tell the story, and we report them honestly.

Languages

FilmiFire publishes content in English and Hindi to serve the widest possible audience across India and the global Indian diaspora.
